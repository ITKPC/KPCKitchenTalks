Support

Need more help?

Start with someone who works alongside you. Ask another board or committee volunteer who uses the same KPC tools, files or processes. They may have already solved the same problem and can often help quickly.

Ask the KPC Technology Team for additional assistance. Explain what you were trying to do, which device you were using and the exact message you saw.

Protect your account. Never share your password, temporary password, multifactor authentication code or sign-in approval number.
